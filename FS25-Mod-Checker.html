FS25 Mod Checker

Finds broken Farming Simulator 25 mods before the game does. Nothing leaves your PC.

Check your mods without loading the game

Pick your FarmingSimulator2025 folder. It's usually Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025. The checker reads your mods, your saves and the game's log.txt, all on this PC. It never changes or uploads anything.

You can also drag a folder or some zips onto this box. Your browser may ask to let this page view the folder; that's read-only.
